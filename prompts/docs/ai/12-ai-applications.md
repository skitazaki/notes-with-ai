Write a concise reference page titled:
"AI Applications"

You are a senior AI product architect and technical writer explaining the layer where AI capabilities become concrete user-facing or operational solutions.

Audience:

- Product engineers, solution architects, AI engineers, technical leaders, and senior developers
- Readers who want to understand how AI shows up as real software and business capability

Purpose:

- Define AI applications as the applied solution layer of the AI landscape
- Explain the main categories of AI-enabled products and workflows
- Show how application value depends on the coordination of models, data, engineering, infrastructure, and governance

Core message:

AI applications are where model capability becomes user value, operational leverage, or domain-specific automation through careful integration with workflows, interfaces, and controls.

Scope:

- Cover major application patterns such as search, copilots, assistants, workflow automation, decision support, robotics, scientific discovery, and domain-specific operational systems
- Explain application design in terms of tasks, users, constraints, interfaces, and approval boundaries
- Clarify how applications differ from underlying models and platforms

Tone and style:

- Neutral, practical, and use-case-oriented
- Focus on durable categories rather than trend lists
- Suitable for technical documentation rather than marketing copy

Structure:

1. Definition
   - Define AI applications as systems that apply AI capability to a concrete user or business problem.

2. Why the application layer matters
   - Explain why user value is determined by workflow fit, controls, and system integration, not only by model quality.

3. Major application categories
   - Search and retrieval
   - Assistants and copilots
   - Content generation
   - Workflow automation
   - Decision support
   - Robotics and embodied systems
   - Scientific and industrial applications

4. Common design dimensions
   - User interaction
   - Context and grounding
   - Approval and oversight
   - Reliability and safety
   - Measurement of value

5. Domain variation
   - Explain how application design changes across software, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, customer support, and research settings.

6. Relationship to the rest of the AI stack
   - Connect applications back to foundation models, data, infrastructure, engineering, operations, and governance.

Output requirements:

- Write the page in Markdown.
- Include frontmatter with the page title and `weight: 12`.
- Use headings and one concise table mapping application categories to primary design concerns.

Constraints:

- Do not make the page a catalog of products.
- Do not reduce applications to chatbots only.
- Do not provide domain-specific implementation playbooks.
