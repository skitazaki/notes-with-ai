Write a concise reference page titled:
"Enterprise AI"

You are a senior enterprise architect and AI strategy writer explaining how organizations adopt, govern, integrate, and scale AI capabilities across business and technical environments.

Audience:

- Enterprise architects, platform leaders, senior engineers, security leaders, and technology decision-makers
- Readers who need a stable conceptual model for organizational AI adoption at scale

Purpose:

- Define enterprise AI as the organizational and architectural layer of AI adoption
- Explain the platform, governance, integration, and operating-model concerns unique to large organizations
- Clarify how enterprise priorities differ from isolated prototypes or narrow product experiments

Core message:

Enterprise AI is the discipline of turning isolated model capabilities into governed, integrated, secure, and economically sustainable organizational systems.

Scope:

- Cover AI platforms, enterprise integration, identity and access, security, cost management, procurement, operating models, and organizational adoption patterns
- Explain the need for shared services and policy controls without erasing domain-level autonomy
- Connect enterprise architecture concerns to AI engineering and responsible AI

Tone and style:

- Neutral, strategic, and technically grounded
- Practical for large-organization readers
- Avoid management-fashion language

Structure:

1. Definition
   - Define enterprise AI as AI adoption viewed through organization-wide architecture, governance, and operating models.

2. Why enterprise AI matters
   - Explain why prototypes do not automatically scale into durable organizational capability.

3. Core enterprise concerns
   - Platform strategy
   - Integration patterns
   - Identity and access
   - Security and compliance
   - Cost and capacity governance
   - Operating model and ownership

4. Centralization and federation
   - Explain how shared platforms and domain autonomy must be balanced.

5. Common failure patterns
   - Fragmented tooling
   - Shadow AI usage
   - Inconsistent controls
   - Unclear ownership
   - Unmanaged costs

6. Relationship to applications
   - Explain how enterprise AI creates the conditions for many domain-specific AI applications.

Output requirements:

- Write the page in Markdown.
- Include frontmatter with the page title and `weight: 11`.
- Use headings and one concise table mapping enterprise concerns to architectural consequences.

Constraints:

- Do not write a business strategy presentation.
- Do not focus on a single industry or vendor stack.
- Do not provide an implementation roadmap or organizational change program.
