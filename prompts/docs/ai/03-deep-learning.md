Write a concise reference page titled:
"Deep Learning"

You are a senior AI researcher and technical writer explaining deep learning as the family of neural approaches that learn layered representations from data.

Audience:

- AI engineers, data scientists, software architects, and technically fluent leaders
- Readers who want a durable conceptual understanding of deep learning’s role in modern AI

Purpose:

- Define deep learning and its place within machine learning
- Explain the major neural architecture families and what problems they address
- Connect representation learning and scale to the emergence of modern AI systems

Core message:

Deep learning matters because layered neural architectures can learn rich internal representations that make perception, generation, and sequence modeling practical at large scale.

Scope:

- Cover neural networks, representation learning, backpropagation, and scale at a conceptual level
- Explain CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs, transformers, autoencoders, and diffusion models as major architecture families
- Clarify how deep learning differs from broader machine learning methods

Tone and style:

- Neutral, technically grounded, and concept-first
- Avoid unnecessary math
- Explain architectural roles rather than teaching model implementation

Structure:

1. Definition
   - Define deep learning as machine learning built on multilayer neural architectures.

2. Why deep learning changed the field
   - Explain representation learning, scale, and the impact of data and compute.

3. Core concepts
   - Parameters
   - Training signals
   - Hidden representations
   - Generalization and scaling

4. Major architecture families
   - CNNs for spatial signals
   - RNNs and LSTMs for sequences
   - Transformers for attention-based sequence and multimodal learning
   - Diffusion and generative architectures

5. Strengths and limits
   - Data hunger
   - Compute intensity
   - Opaqueness and interpretability limits
   - Transfer and reuse benefits

6. Relationship to foundation models
   - Explain why foundation models are built on deep learning rather than replacing it.

Output requirements:

- Write the page in Markdown.
- Include frontmatter with the page title and `weight: 3`.
- Use headings, short paragraphs, and a concise architecture comparison table.

Constraints:

- Do not derive equations or explain optimization mechanics in depth.
- Do not turn the page into a chronology of benchmark wins.
- Do not treat transformers as the only meaningful deep-learning architecture.
