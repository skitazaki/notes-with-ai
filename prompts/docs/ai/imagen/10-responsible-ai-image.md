# Image Generation Prompt - Responsible AI

Create a publication-quality conceptual illustration titled:

"Responsible AI"

Purpose:

Help readers see responsible AI as a system-wide discipline that applies across the lifecycle rather than as a final compliance checkpoint.

Core message:

Responsible AI surrounds data, models, deployment, and operations with trust, control, and accountability mechanisms.

Composition:

- Place a simple horizontal lifecycle in the center:
  - Data
  - Model
  - Deployment
  - Operation
- Surround or overlay that lifecycle with six equally visible control domains:
  - Fairness and Bias
  - Explainability and Transparency
  - Privacy and Data Rights
  - Security and Misuse Resistance
  - Safety and Human Control
  - Compliance and Accountability
- Use a frame, shield lattice, or trust boundary motif so the controls feel continuous across all stages.

Style:

- clean editorial infographic
- vector-style technical illustration
- light background
- balanced use of calm governance colors with one trust accent
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- readable at article width

Do:

- make cross-cutting control visually obvious
- keep all six control domains visible and balanced
- emphasize lifecycle coverage rather than legal formality

Do not:

- make the image feel punitive or bureaucratic
- depict compliance as the only concern
- overload the image with dense policy text or legal references
