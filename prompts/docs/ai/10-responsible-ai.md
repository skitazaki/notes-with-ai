Write a concise reference page titled:
"Responsible AI"

You are a senior AI governance architect and technical writer explaining how organizations make AI systems lawful, safe, fair, secure, and reviewable across their lifecycle.

Audience:

- Architects, AI engineers, risk leaders, platform teams, security leaders, and senior technical decision-makers
- Readers who need a practical but non-legalistic framework for AI trust and control

Purpose:

- Define responsible AI as a cross-cutting governance and design discipline
- Explain the major trust, safety, and accountability concerns in AI systems
- Show how governance applies across data, models, applications, and operations

Core message:

Responsible AI is not a final compliance checkpoint. It is a system-wide discipline that shapes design, deployment, monitoring, and accountability from the beginning.

Scope:

- Cover fairness, explainability, privacy, security, compliance, governance, auditability, human oversight, and AI safety at a high level
- Explain how these concerns appear differently across predictive ML, generative AI, and autonomous or agentic systems
- Clarify the relationship between organizational policy and technical controls

Tone and style:

- Neutral, rigorous, and practical
- Governance-aware without becoming policy jargon
- Suitable for durable technical documentation

Structure:

1. Definition
   - Define responsible AI as the discipline of constraining AI systems so that they remain trustworthy and accountable.

2. Why it matters
   - Explain the technical, legal, operational, and societal consequences of poorly governed AI systems.

3. Core concern areas
   - Fairness and bias
   - Explainability and transparency
   - Privacy and data rights
   - Security and misuse resistance
   - Safety and human control
   - Compliance and accountability

4. Lifecycle application
   - Data selection
   - Model design and adaptation
   - Deployment controls
   - Monitoring and incident response

5. Organizational operating model
   - Explain roles, review gates, and decision ownership at a high level.

6. Relationship to enterprise AI
   - Clarify how responsible AI supports platform strategy, procurement, and organizational trust.

Output requirements:

- Write the page in Markdown.
- Include frontmatter with the page title and `weight: 10`.
- Use headings and one concise table mapping risk areas to representative controls.

Constraints:

- Do not write the page as a legal memo.
- Do not reduce responsible AI to fairness alone.
- Do not provide policy boilerplate or jurisdiction-specific compliance checklists.
