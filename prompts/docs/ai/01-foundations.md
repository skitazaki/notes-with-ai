Write a concise reference page titled:
"Foundations"

You are a senior AI architect and technical writer explaining the conceptual roots of Artificial Intelligence and the main schools of thought that still shape modern systems.

Audience:

- Software architects, AI engineers, data engineers, and senior developers
- Technical leaders who need a stable conceptual map before diving into specific model families or products

Purpose:

- Define the foundational ideas behind AI as a field
- Clarify the relationship between AI, machine learning, and deep learning
- Explain why historical paradigms still matter when interpreting current systems

Core message:

Modern AI makes more sense when it is understood as an accumulation of several traditions rather than as a single recent breakthrough.

Scope:

- Cover the field-level meaning of AI
- Explain symbolic AI, statistical learning, probabilistic reasoning, search, optimization, and neural approaches at a high level
- Show how these traditions differ in assumptions, strengths, and limits
- Position machine learning and deep learning as subsets within the larger AI landscape

Tone and style:

- Neutral, precise, and concept-first
- Practical for experienced technical readers
- Historically aware without becoming an academic survey

Structure:

1. Definition
   - Define AI as a broad discipline concerned with building systems that perceive, reason, learn, generate, or act.

2. Why foundations matter
   - Explain why teams make better design and governance choices when they distinguish the major traditions inside AI.

3. Core traditions
   - Symbolic and rule-based AI
   - Search and planning
   - Probabilistic and statistical methods
   - Machine learning
   - Neural and connectionist approaches

4. AI vs. machine learning vs. deep learning
   - Include a simple comparison table.

5. Enduring tensions
   - Generality vs. specialization
   - Data-driven learning vs. explicit rules
   - Prediction vs. reasoning
   - Capability vs. interpretability

6. Why this foundation still matters now
   - Connect the historical ideas to modern systems such as foundation models, agents, and hybrid architectures.

Output requirements:

- Write the page in Markdown.
- Include frontmatter with the page title and `weight: 1`.
- Use hierarchical headings, short explanatory paragraphs, and one concise comparison table.

Constraints:

- Do not turn the page into a full history of AI.
- Do not include implementation steps or vendor comparisons.
- Do not over-focus on current products or market cycles.
