# Image Generation Prompt — Data Ownership and Stewardship

Create a polished 1536 × 1024 pixels technical infographic titled **Data Ownership & Stewardship**.

## Purpose and Core Message

Show two complementary governance mechanisms rather than a corporate hierarchy:

- **Data Owner** — accountable authority for decisions, approvals, expectations, and risk acceptance.
- **Data Steward** — continuous governance practice through maintenance, coordination, recommendations, and escalation.

Both roles interact directly with a shared **Governed Data Context** containing **Domain**, **Dataset**, and **Data Product**. Stewardship is not subordinate administrative support. Assigning operational work to the steward does not transfer owner accountability; ownership without active stewardship becomes nominal.

## Composition

Use two equal, side-by-side role cards above or beside one shared governed-data area. Give Data Owner and Data Steward equal visual weight, distinct accent colors, and no reporting line between them.

- From Data Owner to the governed-data area, show **Decisions & Expectations**.
- Around Data Steward and the governed-data area, show a continuous-practice loop labeled **Maintain · Coordinate · Monitor**.
- From Data Steward toward Data Owner, show **Recommend & Escalate**.
- From Data Owner toward Data Steward, show **Decide & Set Direction**.

The two-way exchange should communicate decision preparation, escalation, accountable decisions, and their propagation into ongoing practice. Avoid a top-down org chart, delegation tree, or assistant relationship.

Inside the shared data area, show three equal compact elements: **Domain**, **Dataset**, and **Data Product**. Use restrained icons for authority/decision, stewardship/metadata, domain, dataset, and data product.

## Required Text

Use only these exact labels:

- **Data Ownership & Stewardship**
- **Data Owner**
- **Accountable authority**
- **Data Steward**
- **Continuous governance practice**
- **Decisions & Expectations**
- **Recommend & Escalate**
- **Decide & Set Direction**
- **Maintain · Coordinate · Monitor**
- **Governed Data Context**
- **Domain**
- **Dataset**
- **Data Product**

## Style and Output

- Clean editorial infographic matching the Notes with AI governance illustrations.
- Warm off-white background, dark navy typography and outlines, teal and blue accents, restrained violet for escalation.
- Crisp vector-style geometry, rounded cards, consistent flat icons, balanced whitespace, solid connectors, and legible typography.
- Vendor-neutral, timeless, no product branding.
- 1536 × 1024 pixels, 3:2 landscape, high-quality raster for a Hugo page resource.

## Do Not

- Do not place Data Owner above Data Steward or connect them with a one-way delegation arrow.
- Do not label the steward as an assistant, administrator, or support role.
- Do not use a RACI matrix or add producers, consumers, platform teams, committees, job titles, or extra prose.
- Do not imply that the steward accepts risk or makes decisions beyond delegated authority.
- Do not use a simple linear pipeline, crossed connectors, dotted lines, photos, 3D rendering, logos, or watermarks.
