# Image Generation Prompt — Governance Principles and Operating Model

Create a polished 1536 × 1024 pixels technical infographic titled **Governance Principles & Operating Model**.

## Purpose

Help readers understand an operating model as an arrangement of four peer foundation concepts, governance forums, escalation paths, and decision records. Make the diagram visibly different from a lifecycle, rule hierarchy, or control pipeline.

## Core Message

Accountability, Decision Rights, Authority, and Scope are peer foundations of a governance operating model. Forums exercise those foundations at different organizational scopes, unresolved matters can be escalated, and outcomes are retained in a Decision Record. Centralized, Federated, and Hybrid are equally valid structural choices.

## Composition

Use one foundation row above a forum row, followed by a compact decision-record element and footer.

### 1. Foundations

Place a wide container across the upper portion labeled **Foundations**. Inside it, arrange four equal cards in one horizontal row:

- **Accountability** — person or ownership icon
- **Decision Rights** — branching decision or mandate icon
- **Authority** — mandate or gavel icon
- **Scope** — boundary or target icon

All four cards must have identical size, position in the hierarchy, color weight, and typography. Do not place one inside another.

### 2. Decision Forums and Escalation

Place a band labeled **Governance Forums** below Foundations. Inside it, arrange three equally sized cards from left to right:

1. **Domain Forum**
2. **Cross-domain Council**
3. **Enterprise Forum**

Connect Domain Forum rightward to Cross-domain Council and Cross-domain Council rightward to Enterprise Forum using solid arrows. Place the label **Escalation** above the arrows.

Use restrained vertical connectors from the Foundations container to the Governance Forums band, showing that all four foundations apply across all forum scopes.

### 3. Decision Record

Place one smaller independent card labeled **Decision Record** beneath the Governance Forums band. Connect all three forums to it with restrained solid lines. Use a document-and-pencil icon. The Decision Record is an output of forum decisions, not a foundation and not another forum.

### 4. Operating-Model Choices

Place three equal cards in one horizontal row across the bottom:

- **Centralized**
- **Federated**
- **Hybrid**

Give all three cards equal visual weight. Do not connect them with arrows or arrange them as a progression.

## Visual Hierarchy

- Title at the top
- Foundations row and Governance Forums row as the dominant content
- Four foundation cards visibly equal
- Escalation path clear but secondary to the forum labels
- Decision Record separate and visually subordinate to the forums
- Operating-model choices compact and visually subordinate
- Large, readable labels with no supporting paragraphs

## Style

- Clean editorial infographic for technical documentation
- Crisp vector-style shapes, simple geometry, and generous whitespace
- Warm off-white background with dark navy typography and outlines
- Teal for governance decisions, blue for forum and escalation elements, and restrained slate accents
- Consistent flat icon family, rounded rectangular containers, and uniform line weight
- High-contrast typography readable at documentation-column width
- Solid connectors only
- Vendor-neutral and timeless, with no product branding

## Output

- 1536 × 1024 pixels
- 3:2 landscape aspect ratio
- High-quality raster image suitable for use as a Hugo page resource

Use only these exact labels:

- **Governance Principles & Operating Model**
- **Foundations**
- **Decision Rights**
- **Accountability**
- **Authority**
- **Scope**
- **Decision Record**

## Do

- Make the foundation row clearly different from the forum and escalation row.
- Use consistent people, decision, mandate, boundary, record, and forum icons.
- Show Accountability, Decision Rights, Authority, and Scope as peers.
- Show Decision Record only as an output from governance forums.
- Preserve generous whitespace around the three main areas.
- Render all required text exactly as written.

## Do Not

- Show Strategy, Policies, Controls, Evidence, or Review stages.
- Add a strategy-to-review loop, policy hierarchy, control pipeline, or circular process.
- Place Accountability, Authority, Scope, or Decision Record inside Decision Rights.
- Place Decision Record in the Foundations row.
- Use maturity arrows or any visual ranking among Centralized, Federated, and Hybrid.
- Use dotted lines, decorative particles, ornamental backgrounds, glossy 3D rendering, stock photography, extra text, tiny labels, logos, or watermarks.
