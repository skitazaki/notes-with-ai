# Image Generation Prompt — From Quality Dimension to Operational Control

Create a polished 1536 × 1024 pixel technical infographic titled **From Quality Dimension to Operational Control**.

## Purpose and Core Message

Distinguish the concepts commonly conflated in data quality work. Show how a dimension is measured, evaluated, and governed, and how observed failures lead to operational response.

## Composition

Use three equal, clearly separated rectangular group panels in one horizontal row. The panels must occupy the same height and width and align to one shared top and bottom edge.

From left to right, place the panels in this exact order:

1. **Define & Measure**
2. **Evaluate**
3. **Operate**

Place one short, horizontal, right-facing arrow in each gutter between adjacent group panels. Center each arrow vertically in the gutter. The arrows connect the three groups only; they must not touch or connect individual cards.

Inside the **Define & Measure** panel, stack two equal cards vertically in this exact top-to-bottom order:

1. **Dimension**
2. **Metric**

Inside the **Evaluate** panel, stack three equal cards vertically in this exact top-to-bottom order:

1. **Rule**
2. **Constraint**
3. **Check**

Inside the **Operate** panel, stack three equal cards vertically in this exact top-to-bottom order:

1. **Signal**
2. **Incident**
3. **SLA**

Use equal internal spacing and center every card within its group panel. Do not draw any lines or arrows between cards. Group membership, not card-to-card wiring, expresses the relationship among concepts.

Treat the interior as three shared horizontal card rows. Align **Dimension**, **Rule**, and **Signal** on row 1; **Metric**, **Constraint**, and **Incident** on row 2; and **Check** and **SLA** on row 3. Leave row 3 of **Define & Measure** intentionally empty. All eight cards must have exactly the same width and height.

Add one short descriptor inside each card. Use square corners or a very small 4 px corner radius only.

## Exact Card Content

- **Dimension** — What aspect matters
- **Metric** — How it is measured
- **Check** — How it is evaluated
- **Constraint** — What the data model enforces
- **Rule** — What condition is acceptable
- **Signal** — What behavior is observed
- **Incident** — What failure requires response
- **SLA** — What service objective applies

## Style and Output

- Clean flat-vector editorial infographic; professional enterprise architecture aesthetic
- Warm off-white background; dark navy typography; teal and blue accents; restrained amber for Incident
- Crisp rectangular rules, consistent line weight, simple line icons, strong alignment, ample whitespace
- High legibility at article width; correct English spelling; no text smaller than 26 px at full resolution
- 1536 × 1024 pixels, exactly 3:2 landscape, high-quality raster

## Required Text

Use only the title, these three group headings, and the concept labels and descriptors specified above. Render every phrase exactly once.

## Do Not

- Do not use circles, rings, arcs, capsules, pill shapes, heavily rounded cards, or curved connectors.
- Do not merge cards or present all eight concepts as one linear lifecycle.
- Do not place cards between columns, overlap cards, stagger rows, or use diagonal positioning.
- Do not draw vertical, diagonal, branching, elbow-shaped, or card-to-card connectors.
- Do not connect **Rule** or **Constraint** directly to **Metric** or **Check**.
- Do not connect **SLA** directly to **Signal** or **Incident**.
- Do not add examples, legends, numbering, decorative prose, or labels not specified above.
- Do not duplicate, omit, abbreviate, or paraphrase any required text.
- Do not use gradients, shadows, glossy 3D, photography, decorative particles, logos, or watermarks.
