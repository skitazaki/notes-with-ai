# Image Generation Prompt — Data Quality Dimensions

Create a polished 1536 × 1024 pixel technical infographic titled **Data Quality Dimensions**.

## Purpose and Core Message

Show a stable core of six data quality dimensions and five optional groups that extend the model for particular contexts. The extensions depend on the core, but they are peers; they are not sequential stages or maturity levels.

## Composition

Use a strict, rectilinear three-column grid on a 3:2 landscape canvas.

- Make the center column a clearly bounded vertical rectangle titled **Core Dimensions**. Inside it, place six equal rectangular cells in a 2 × 3 grid: **Accuracy**, **Completeness**, **Consistency**, **Timeliness**, **Uniqueness**, and **Validity**.
- Place **Structural Extensions** and **Runtime Extensions** as two separate rectangular panels in the left column.
- Place **Semantic Extensions** and **AI Extensions** as two separate rectangular panels in the right column.
- Place **Governance Extensions** as one wide rectangular panel below the center column, aligned to the same grid.
- Connect each extension panel to the core with one short, straight, thin solid line. Do not connect extension panels to one another.
- Keep every panel fully separated with generous whitespace. Use square corners or a very small 4 px corner radius only.

The composition must read as a stable center with optional surrounding concerns, not as a flower, wheel, orbit, cycle, target, or Venn diagram.

## Panel Details

Use one simple line icon and the listed examples in each extension panel:

- **Structural Extensions** — schema/relationship icon; **Referential Integrity**, **Entity Integrity**, **Conformity**
- **Runtime Extensions** — monitoring icon; **Freshness**, **Volume Anomalies**, **Distribution Drift**, **Pipeline Reliability**
- **Semantic Extensions** — meaning/people icon; **Relevance**, **Interpretability**, **Understandability**, **Accessibility**
- **Governance Extensions** — shield/lineage icon; **Traceability**, **Auditability**, **Policy Compliance**, **Retention Compliance**
- **AI Extensions** — model/network icon; **Representativeness**, **Bias & Fairness**, **Feature Stability**, **Reproducibility**

## Style and Output

- Clean flat-vector editorial infographic; professional enterprise architecture aesthetic
- Warm off-white background; dark navy typography; restrained teal, blue, green, amber, and violet accents
- Crisp rectangular rules, consistent line weight, simple line icons, strong alignment, ample whitespace
- High legibility at article width; correct English spelling; no text smaller than 24 px at full resolution
- 1536 × 1024 pixels, exactly 3:2 landscape, high-quality raster
