# Image Generation Prompt - Master Data Management Executive Summary

Create a publication-quality conceptual illustration titled:

"Master Data Management (MDM)"

Subtitle: Shared identity for core business entities across systems.

Purpose:

Introduce MDM as the discipline that reconciles fragmented representations of the same business entities across enterprise systems.
The image should make it clear that MDM is not just storage consolidation. It is coordinated identity, stewardship, and synchronization across multiple operational systems.

Style:

- clean editorial infographic
- modern technical documentation aesthetic
- crisp vector-style shapes
- light background
- restrained enterprise palette with 5 to 6 coordinated colors
- readable labels
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- suitable for a docs section hero image
- concept-first, not product-marketing style
- vendor-neutral, with no logos or product branding

Composition:

Show several enterprise systems arranged around a central mastered entity hub.
Use source systems such as:

- CRM
- ERP
- Billing
- Support
- Warehouse or Fulfillment

In each source system, depict slightly different records for the same customer or product, using differing IDs, names, or statuses to suggest fragmentation.
At the center, show an MDM hub or mastered entity layer that resolves these fragmented records into one coherent business entity.

Include small but readable cues for:

- duplicate or conflicting source records
- identity resolution or matching
- stewardship or review
- synchronization back to consuming systems

Use subtle directional arrows to show that information flows into the MDM layer for matching and governance, and that curated identity flows outward for shared use.

Central message:

MDM creates a trusted shared identity for core business entities across disconnected systems.
It reduces fragmentation without pretending the enterprise operates as one monolithic database.

Small caption:

"One business entity, many source records, one mastered view."

Do:

- make the same entity visibly appear in multiple systems under slightly different forms
- emphasize reconciliation, stewardship, and shared meaning
- keep the systems abstract but recognizably enterprise-oriented
- make the central mastered entity feel authoritative but not physically dominant over every system
- show that MDM coordinates systems rather than replacing every application

Do not:

- turn the image into a literal vendor product diagram
- make the central hub look like a single database that owns all enterprise data
- overload the figure with tiny field names or dense tables
- use decorative business clip art
- imply that MDM is only for analytics

Audience:

- enterprise architects
- data architects
- data engineers
- governance practitioners
- senior software engineers
